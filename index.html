Our Love Story

How Did You Meet?

I'd honestly say our meeting was divinely orchestrated.

Before we met, my phone was stolen in a taxi. At the time, it felt like one of those frustrating moments you wish never happened. Looking back now, I believe God was simply putting the right pieces in place.

While trying to replace my sim, I ended up at an MTN office. That's where I first saw her. She walked past me, and I immediately noticed how beautiful and graceful she was. I secretly hoped she'd be the customer service representative assigned to me, and she was.

We talked, laughed, and for one of the very few times in my life, I asked a woman for her number. She said yes.

From that day, we spoke every single day. Through work, long late-night conversations, and every season of life, we kept choosing each other. When we finally had our first date — a simple picnic in the park — I knew she was the woman I wanted to spend forever with.

Looking back now, every detour, every challenge, and even a stolen phone led me to her. I truly believe God wrote our story long before we met.

We met at my workplace, he had come in to retrieve his missing SIM card, and I honestly didn't notice him when he first walked in. It wasn't until I called out, "Next customer, please!" and he was referred to my desk that I finally looked up.

And there he was... looking all clean and handsome, with skin as smooth as milk. 😄 I had to quickly compose myself and switch back into professional mode.

Then something unexpected happened—the network at the office went down. What should have been a quick customer interaction suddenly turned into an unplanned conversation. We talked, laughed, and got to know each other while waiting for the system to come back on.

Before he left, he asked for my number, saying it was "just in case" his SIM card didn't work. Well... I guess the SIM card worked.

From that day on, we spoke every single day. One conversation became many, friendship grew into something beautiful, and before we knew it, we had found our forever.

Looking back now, I can confidently say it was divinely designed. What looked like an ordinary workday, a missing SIM card, and even a network outage were all pieces of God's perfect plan. He orchestrated every detail so our paths would cross at exactly the right time. The rest, as they say, is history... and now we're writing our happily ever after. ❤️

Who said "I love you" first?

I did.

I've never really been the "I love you first" type. I've always been someone who led more with my head than my heart.

But she changed that. Her kindness, her sincerity, and the way she made me feel completely safe broke down every wall I had built. Loving her became the easiest and most natural thing in the world, and saying "I love you" first felt like the only right thing to do.

He did.

Mention two things that made you fall more in love

First, her nurturing heart.

She naturally takes care of people. Whether it's family, friends, or me, she's always asking if everyone is okay, making sure everyone feels loved and cared for. She brings warmth wherever she goes, and it's one of the qualities I admire most about her.

Second, her mind.

I'm not just in love with her beauty — I'm deeply in love with the way she thinks. She's incredibly intelligent, wise beyond her years, and always gives thoughtful advice. Watching her grow, excel at work, and solve problems with so much wisdom makes me admire her even more every day.

She has a beautiful heart, a beautiful mind, and yes... the most beautiful smile and body too. But it's the person she is on the inside that makes me fall more in love with her every single day.

One of the things I admire most about him is how consistent he is. From the very beginning, he has been intentional, dependable, and true to his word, and that gives me so much peace. He's also incredibly soft-spoken, which is so calming. He has this beautiful way of anticipating my needs before I even express them. Whether it's checking in on me after a long day, remembering the little things that matter to me, or doing thoughtful acts of kindness without being asked, he constantly makes me feel seen. I genuinely love the way he loves me, he's patient, selfless, intentional, and reassuring.

Another thing that makes me fall even more in love with him is his kind and caring heart. He has one of the most genuine hearts I've ever known. He loves with so much sincerity and makes it so easy to feel safe with him. He celebrates my wins as though they were his own, stands by me through every challenge, and never fails to remind me that I'm not alone. With him, I feel deeply loved, valued, respected, and supported every single day. His love isn't just something he says, it's something he lives out consistently through his words, his actions, and the way he chooses me every single day.
